## Recurring programmes and content (good candidates for site sections and categories)
- **Jeudi Propre**: weekly neighbourhood clean-up; the most frequent post type.
- **Vacances utiles**: holiday internships for students at the Mairie. **Vacances informatiques / PBCPN**: digital scholarship programme, 8th edition in 2026.
- **Noël pour Tous**: children's Christmas, 4th edition, with the Fondation NIAT.
- Youth and jobs: ORFEV Jeunes, JEME, Youth Connekt, the Bureau Municipal de l'Emploi.
- Infrastructure (BIP projects): municipal stadium phase 3, Ngam bridge, Nkong bridge, "La Braise" market halls and shopping centre, classrooms (Bitchoua, Peup-Bangoua), solar street lighting, "Bangangté ville fontaine" water points, south entrance via Batela.
- Social action: assistance files, people with disabilities, school kits for 59 public primary schools.
- Participatory development: **village committees** installed (Tchoudim, Bantoum 3) [134][172].
- Cooperation: Berlin Charlottenburg (youth exchange), a Luxembourg partner, KfW PRODESV 3 (shortlisted), the German Embassy, FEICOM, UN-Habitat, HYSACAM.
- Official notices: **communiqués** (market traders, allottees), **avis d'appel à candidature** (leisure park, green space) [193][194], traffic and parking plans [133], alerts (African swine fever [115], weather [51]), a citizen survey [92].
- Institutional: council sessions, official ceremonies (20 May, 1 May, Women's Day, Youth Day), obituaries, media coverage (CRTV, Cameroon Tribune, Equinoxe).
- Sport: AS Nzuimanto (U16 girls' national basketball champions) [103], holiday championships, Tour cycliste stage 5 [121], the Mairie winning the Tournoi de l'Unité.
